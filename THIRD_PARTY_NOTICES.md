The MIT license covers original Valora code. Third-party materials retain their

Google, Dribbble, CRED, or other third-party names, marks or screenshots.

- DM Sans and Manrope fonts include their SIL Open Font License files in
  `mobile/assets/fonts` and `landing/public/assets/fonts`.
- Gemma notices remain in `mobile/assets/licenses`. This variant does not
  download or execute Gemma by default and does not distribute its weights.
- NVIDIA Nemotron is invoked as a hosted service; weights are not bundled.
- Dependencies retain their upstream licenses; Flutter's license registry is
  available through its standard license APIs.
- Third-party static design references were removed from the working tree.
  Historical Git commits may still contain them; deleting files does not establish
  redistribution permission for those historical assets.
- Provenance/redistribution rights for landing images must be confirmed before
  publishing the landing assets. The Android demo does not bundle these images.

Public repository and video publication remain blocked on this asset review.

Flutter Markdown Plus and Dart markdown are used for formatted assistant replies. Their package license notices are bundled through Flutter's package-license mechanism. Research summaries are original short notes with citation links; publication text is not relicensed under this repository's MIT license.

The speech_to_text package and its platform dependencies provide system speech recognition. Their package license notices are included through Flutter's license mechanism. System speech recognition is provided by the device/platform service, not the Nebius model.
